## Copy rules

- Prefer observable facts over quality adjectives.
- Use `since 1995` instead of recalculating a year count throughout the codebase.
- Do not claim cheapest, best, leading, guaranteed, certified or specialist accreditation unless separately approved.
- Do not imply structural engineering, heritage-consultant or council-approval services are performed by Romans when a third party is required.
- Generated images cannot be presented as completed Romans work.
