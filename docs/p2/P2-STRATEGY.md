# Romans Building Services P2 Strategy

## Positioning

Romans is the Sydney masonry and remedial specialist for owners who need the problem diagnosed honestly, repaired with the right material and method, and handled by the person accountable for the result.

The site should not compete on breadth, visual spectacle or lowest price. It should compete on judgment, workmanship, accountability and evidence.

## Primary audience

1. Sydney homeowners with visible cracking, failed mortar, damaged stone, water ingress or an ageing masonry structure.
2. Heritage property owners who care about preserving original material and character.
3. Strata managers, architects and builders who need a reliable specialist to assess or execute remedial masonry work.

## Customer decision sequence

1. Is this the kind of problem Romans handles?
2. Can I trust the diagnosis and workmanship?
3. Have they completed comparable work?
4. Who will inspect and perform the work?
5. What happens if I make contact?
6. Can I call now or show them the problem without writing a long brief?

## Homepage evidence skeleton

1. **Hero:** Sydney masonry and remedial work since 1995, with a direct quote action and project-proof action.
2. **Proof bar:** Only ledger-approved tenure, operating model, service area and credential claims.
3. **Problem navigator:** Route by what the visitor sees, not internal service taxonomy.
4. **Featured proof:** Three approved projects showing problem, intervention and outcome with direct case-study links.
5. **Founder standard:** Minas as the accountable assessor and working specialist, supported by authentic footage.
6. **Capabilities:** A concise three-part view of heritage, masonry and structural/remedial work. No duplicated directory grid.
7. **Process:** One compact explanation from first contact to completed work.
8. **Questions:** The smallest set of high-impact objections supported by approved facts.
9. **Conversion:** Show the problem through the quote survey or call Minas.
10. **Footer:** Preserve broader services, areas, Learn and case-study discovery.

## Aesthetic contract

### Preserve

- The real Romans logo.
- Authentic on-site founder and project imagery.
- Deep navy as the authority colour.
- Amber as a deliberate action and craft accent.
- Editorial serif headlines paired with plain, readable body copy.
- The current owner-led, plain-English voice.

### Change

- Reduce repeated navy cards and generic directory grids.
- Replace isolated stacked sections with a continuous editorial rhythm.
- Use larger evidence images with project-specific captions and links.
- Tighten mobile content to a decision journey rather than a complete sitemap.
- Give actions consistent priority and wording.
- Use borders, rules, labels and material textures sparingly instead of generic shadows.

### Reject

- Generic AI construction imagery.
- Abstract 3D objects without narrative meaning.
- Stock-photo polish that weakens proof.
- Unsupported superlatives, invented testimonials or new quantitative claims.
- Autoplay media competing with the first render.
- Animation that blocks reading, quote access or reduced-motion users.

## Motion roles

Motion may clarify only one of these roles:

1. Reveal a diagnostic sequence.
2. Connect problem, intervention and finished outcome.
3. Emphasise the hand and material in authentic footage.
4. Preserve orientation between sections.

Implementation should prefer lightweight CSS and IntersectionObserver reveals. A large animation library is allowed only if a specific interaction cannot be delivered accessibly and within the performance budget.

## Scope boundaries

- Preserve all existing public routes and their prerendering.
- Preserve quote, call tracking, SEO metadata, schema and real 404 behaviour.
- Homepage P2 does not rewrite every service or area page.
- New generated media is not required because the current proof library is extensive. Any future generated transition asset must be clearly decorative and cannot depict claimed Romans work.
